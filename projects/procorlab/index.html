Procorlab is the website of Procor, a company founded during the pandemic to provide COVID tests and offer a booking service for testing in Madrid. For this project, I developed an e-commerce platform, an appointment booking system, a private client area for customer information, and a blog.
Ensuring a seamless user experience was a key focus of the project, with a clean and intuitive design that made it easy for users to navigate the site and find the information they needed.
The e-commerce platform was designed to be user-friendly and efficient, allowing customers to easily book appointments and purchase COVID tests online.
