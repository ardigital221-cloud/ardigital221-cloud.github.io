La Bamba is a website that I designed and developed for a company that organizes events in Madrid. The site provides information about the different shows organized by the company, including details about the location, date, and artists performing at each occasion.
The company wanted a website that was easy to update, visually appealing, and user-friendly, so I designed a clean, modern site with strong visuals and simple navigation.
Basing the design on a clean and modern aesthetic, I created a visually striking website that effectively communicates the energy and excitement of the events organized by La Bamba.
